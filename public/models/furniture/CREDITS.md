# Furniture model credits

Every `.glb` in this folder is a third-party model under a licence that allows
commercial use without payment. They were optimised (variants/lights removed,
re-rooted to face +Z, upholstery colour maps neutralised for tinting, textures
resized and converted to WebP, Meshopt geometry compression) by
`scripts/furniture/build-models.mjs`. The optimisation is a modification of the
original; the originals are at the source URLs below.
